YOUR INFORMATION STAYS UNDER YOUR CONTROL

Private by design—not just private by promise.

Unlike public AI services, the system operates on a server located inside your organization. Prompts, conversations, uploaded documents and AI-generated responses remain within your company-controlled network. This allows employees to use AI with sensitive operational, customer, financial and technical information without routinely sending that data to an outside AI provider.

CONNECTED TO YOUR COMPANY KNOWLEDGE

Better answers through approved RAG data.

The private AI can also be connected to your company’s approved RAG knowledge base. This produces responses that are more relevant to your organization and can include references to the source material used.

Access can be organized by employee, department or job responsibility. Sales, operations, accounting, human resources, engineering and service teams can each have access to the information appropriate for their work.
